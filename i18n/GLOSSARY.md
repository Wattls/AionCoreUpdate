# Glossary

Terms that must be translated the same way everywhere in AionCore. **Never translate** = keep as written. **Game** = use the name of the official Aion 2 client in your language. **Product** = the AionCore wording we chose for each language.

The maintainer has not approved every product term yet: if you find a better word for your language, open a *Translation suggestion* issue.
